# Mission Reflection

After completing this mission, the cloud platform that impressed me the most was Google Cloud Platform (GCP). I found its services for Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Kubernetes interesting, especially because GCP provides Google Kubernetes Engine for containerized applications. However, I also learned that AWS and Azure have their own strengths and can be better choices depending on what a company needs.

AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similarities because they all provide important cloud services such as virtual machines, object storage, networking, identity management, databases, and Kubernetes. They also allow organizations to scale their resources and run applications without having to maintain all of the physical hardware themselves. The main differences are their services, features, pricing, infrastructure, and areas where each provider is particularly strong.

Business requirements are important when choosing a cloud platform because different organizations have different needs and budgets. For example, a university that already uses Windows Server, Microsoft 365, and Active Directory may benefit more from Azure because of its Microsoft integration. An AI research company may prefer GCP because of its AI and Machine Learning capabilities, while a global e-commerce company may choose AWS because of its wide range of services and global infrastructure.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that choosing a cloud provider is not simply about selecting the most popular platform. I learned that the best choice depends on the client's goals, existing technology, workload, scalability, and other requirements. I also learned how similar services from different providers can be compared and matched.

My GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio has improved because it now contains more organized documentation and evidence of my cloud computing activities. I added research about AWS, Azure, and GCP, comparison tables, client recommendations, a decision matrix, and my KillerCoda Linux investigation. This makes my portfolio more complete and shows the practical knowledge I gained from the activity.
